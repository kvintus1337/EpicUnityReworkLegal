# The Eye of Sauron Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

The Eye of Sauron (“the Bot”) is operated for the Epic Unity Rework Discord server by Sapphire. For questions, privacy requests, or requests to delete applicable stored data, contact `.kvintus` on Discord.

## Purpose

The Bot is used to provide features, automation, and server-management functionality for the Epic Unity Rework Discord server.

## Information the Bot May Process

In order to operate its functions, The Eye of Sauron may process information made available through Discord, including:

- Discord user IDs, usernames, display names, and avatars;
- Discord server, channel, role, message, and interaction IDs;
- Commands, interactions, and other information voluntarily submitted to the Bot;
- Server configuration, role settings, and preferences configured by authorised server administrators;
- Membership information, including join and leave events, when required by an enabled Bot feature;
- Message content only where this is necessary for an enabled feature or where users directly interact with the Bot.

The Bot processes only the information reasonably necessary to provide its available features, operate the server’s automation, prevent abuse, troubleshoot technical problems, and maintain service security.

## How Information Is Used

Information processed by the Bot may be used to:

- Respond to commands and interactions;
- Deliver the Bot’s features and server automation;
- Store and apply server-specific settings;
- Manage roles, permissions, and membership-related functions;
- Detect misuse, investigate errors, and protect the Bot and server from abuse;
- Maintain, improve, and troubleshoot the Bot’s functionality.

## Sharing of Information

The Eye of Sauron does not sell personal data.

Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the Bot through Discord and Sapphire, to comply with applicable law, to protect the security or integrity of the Bot and Epic Unity Rework server, or where a server administrator enables a feature or integration that requires such processing.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Bot’s functionality, preserve server settings, maintain security, resolve technical issues, or comply with legal obligations.

A user may stop new processing by no longer using the Bot. Server administrators may remove the Bot from the server. Users or authorised server representatives may request deletion of applicable stored data by contacting `.kvintus` on Discord. Requests should identify the relevant Discord user ID or server ID where possible.

## Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information processed by the Bot. However, no online service or data transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be made available through the Bot’s help command and the Epic Unity Rework Discord server’s information channel.
